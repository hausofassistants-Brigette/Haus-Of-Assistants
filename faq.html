Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What is Haus Of Assistants?

We provide virtual administration, reception and customer support to help businesses stay organised and save time.

What can you help with?

Email management, appointments, customer enquiries, follow-ups, CRM updates, data entry and general administration.

Do I need to hire you full-time?

No. Our flexible packages give you access to support without the commitment of employing a full-time team member.

How do I send you tasks?

Tasks can be submitted through your client portal or by email. You can include deadlines and priorities so we know what needs attention first.

Can I track my tasks?

Yes. Your client portal allows you to see your tasks and their progress.

How quickly will tasks be completed?

This depends on the task, priority and hours available in your package. We'll work through tasks according to your agreed priorities.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. We take reasonable steps to protect your business and customer information. Please see our Privacy Policy for more information.

Can I change my package?

Yes. If your business needs more or less support, we can discuss changing your package where availability allows.

Do you offer one-off services?

Yes. We can assist with one-off projects such as CRM clean-ups, inbox resets and other administrative tasks.

How do I get started?

Simply get in touch. We'll discuss your business, what you need help with and the best way for us to support you.
